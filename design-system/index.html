Two inks and paper

Cobalt#2148B8Identity, links, structure.
Signal Red#C83232One focal annotation; errors.
Neutral White#FAFAF7Paper and open space.
  • Blue carries roughly 80% of printed coverage; red carries 20%.
  • Give the red plate one role in editorial artwork: dots, annotation, or construction stroke. Keep at least one large quiet area.
  • Halftone belongs in the artwork. Functional text stays clean and readable.
  • For screens, neutral text is allowed. Dark mode uses blue-gray #151A21; link text uses #AFC0ED for contrast. Printed artwork keeps its paper colors.
